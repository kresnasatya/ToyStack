High-contrast mode

Press Ctrl+H to toggle forced colors. Every color on this page is replaced by the browser palette: black background, white text, yellow links, orchid visited links, gold focus rings. Press Ctrl+H again to get the original colors back.

This paragraph is khaki text on gold, set with !important. It is nearly unreadable normally and must become white on black when forced.

This paragraph sets its colors with an inline style attribute, which normally beats every stylesheet. Forced colors still wins, because it runs after inline styles.

Forced: white border, black background, white text.

Hidden note: black on black normally, white on black when forced. If you can read this, the @media (forced-colors: active) query matched.

Author note: black on whitesmoke normally. When forced, the background becomes black, and the rule inside the media query would keep the text black — but forced colors runs last, so the text is white and stays readable. That is the point: the user's palette cannot be defeated, not even by a page that knows about the mode.
