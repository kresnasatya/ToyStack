X-Frame-Options

A page can refuse to be shown inside a frame with the X-Frame-Options response header. The frames below are served by www/ch15/xfo-server.ts; two of them send the header.

DENY — xfo-deny.html (should be blocked)

SAMEORIGIN, cross origin — 127.0.0.1 vs localhost (blocked)

SAMEORIGIN, same origin — served from this host (allowed)

No header — xfo-plain.html (allowed)
