Overlapped box transform animations

The salmon target is static. The blue mover is composited (opacity: 0.999) and slides on and off the target. At composite time the two boxes do not overlap. Correct: blue (later in the document) stays on top of salmon. Buggy: salmon shows through, flickers, or gets dragged along with the animation.
